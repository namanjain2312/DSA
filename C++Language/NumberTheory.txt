   Left Shift (<<)
   The leftshift operator shifts the bits of an integer to the left by a specific number of
   positions (as mentioned). This left shift operation is equivalent to multiplying the integer by
   2 raised to the power of the number of positions shifted. The symbol used to represent the
   left shift operator is <<.
